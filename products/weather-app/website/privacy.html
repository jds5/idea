Your plans stay
on your device.

This describes the current local development build. Dayward has not launched a public service.

In the Android app

Plan names, chosen places and time windows, preferences and forecast baselines are saved in the app’s private storage. Android backups are disabled. Delete a plan in the app, or clear app storage to remove all local data. The app requests internet access; it does not request your location, contacts or notification permission.

Requests to your gateway

Viewing weather sends the selected sample city to the configured gateway. Inspecting or rechecking a plan also sends its time window and preference profile. Plan names and saved forecast baselines are not uploaded. The gateway sees connection metadata such as IP addresses; reverse proxies and infrastructure may log requests according to their configuration.

This website

The static site has no cookies, analytics, advertising scripts, remote fonts, contact forms or account system. The server or hosting provider may process ordinary connection metadata. The local gateway does not implement request logging.

Support and future changes

This build is for local development. Report problems to the person who supplied your build. A public operator, support contact, hosting policy and any future advertising disclosures must be established before a public release.

SOURCES & LIMITS · 30 SEPTEMBER 2026

Keep the uncertainty
in the picture.

Dayward’s first internal build reads NOAA GFS and DWD ICON forecast output through an unmodified, self-hosted Open-Meteo query service. We do not run or train a global weather model.

Credit where it belongs

Weather data: NOAA GFS, DWD ICON, processed by Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Dayward converts hourly values into explicit time intervals and summarises the period you select. These are forecasts, not local observations.

What the build can show

Two models for six city samples, with temperature, hourly rain, wind and gust references when available. Rain is aligned to the hour it covers. Wind and temperature are samples, not continuous measurements. A recent fetch does not establish that every upstream model is fresh.

What it cannot conclude yet

The adapter does not establish the forecast run and native temporal resolution of every point in the stitched series. Automatic recommendations remain unavailable. The app can save a manually selected plan and compare window metrics with a later collected forecast; it cannot claim a calibrated probability or guarantee suitable weather.

Official warnings are not connected. Missing warning data does not mean there is no warning. Month-ahead outlooks, notifications and full global coverage are not available in this build.

Independent by design

Dayward is a working name for an independent project. It is not affiliated with Windy or the weather centres. Monetisation is still being evaluated, and there are no ads or purchases in the internal build.

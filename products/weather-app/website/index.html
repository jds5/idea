THE FIRST SMALL STEP

Built for a real task.
Still an early build.

The internal Android build connects to a self-hosted gateway. Six sample cities, real forecast data, manual time selection and plans stored on your device.

Automatic recommendations are paused while we verify model run times and native hourly data. Official warnings, notifications and month-ahead outlooks are not connected.
